Bar #7

DNA.

Kendrick Lamar

I just win again, then win again, like Wimbledon, I serve Yeah, that's him again, the sound that engine in is like a bird You see fireworks and Corvette tire skrrt the boulevard I know how you work, I know just who you are See, you's a, you's a, you's a— Bitch, your hormones prolly switch inside your DNA Problem is, all that sucker shit inside your DNA Daddy prolly snitched, heritage inside your DNA

Rhyme Scheme

Rapid-fire 3-syllable internal rhyming (/ɪn-?-ɪn/) switching to an aggressive Mono-Rhyme.

Producer Notes

This verse captures the 'Beat Switch' moment. First, Kendrick executes a 'Rapid-Fire Multi,' stacking five distinct 3-syllable rhymes (Win again/Win again/Wimbledon/Him again/Engine in) in just two bars. Then, after the beat drops, he switches to a punchy mono-rhyme, hammering the /ɪ/ sound (Switch/Shit/Snitched) to match the aggression of the production.
