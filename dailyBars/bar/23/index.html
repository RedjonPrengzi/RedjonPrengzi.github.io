Bar #23

98 Freestyle

Big L

Yo, fuck all the glamours and glitz, I plan to get rich I'm from New York and never was a fan of the Knicks And I'm all about expandin' my chips You mad 'cause I was in the van with your bitch with both hands on her tits Corleone hold the throne, that you know in your heart I got style, plus the way that I be flowin' is sharp A while back I used to hustle, sellin' blow in the park Countin' G stacks and rockin' ice that glow in the dark

Rhyme Scheme

Bipolar phonetic stacking of /æ/ and /ɪ/ phonemes transitioning into a long 'O' and rhotic 'AR' mosaic.

Producer Notes

Big L delivers a masterclass in staccato phonetic stacking, utilizing a binary vowel system of /æ/ and /ɪ/ to create a percussive 'Harlem snap' within each bar. By alternating these short, high-frequency vowels, he generates a machine-gun cadence that feels faster than the actual BPM of the track. The second half of the quatrain transitions into a masterful display of rhoticity and long-vowel resonance, where 'flowin' is sharp' and 'blow in the park' function as perfect phonetic mirrors. This verse exemplifies the 'flamboyant' style: a calculated marriage of high-density multis and effortless street bravado.
