Bar #29

Respiration

Black Star (Mos Def)

The new moon rode high in the crown of the metropolis Shinin', like "Who on top of this?" People was tusslin', arguin' and bustlin' Gangstas of Gotham hardcore hustlin' I'm wrestlin' with words and ideas My ears is pricked, seekin' what will transmit The scribes can apply the transcript Yo! This ain't no time where the usual is suitable Tonight alive, let's describe the inscrutable The indisputable

Rhyme Scheme

Triple-syllable mosaic stacking transitioning into a liquid /ʌslɪn/ dactylic chain.

Producer Notes

Mos Def opens with a highly cinematic perspective, personifying the metropolis through a series of fluid, liquid-like rhymes. The transition from the 'metropolis/top of this' mosaic rhyme into the 'tusslin/bustlin/hustlin' chain demonstrates a masterful use of dactylic meter, where the rhythm mimics the urban chaos of the city itself. He elevates the discourse by utilizing technical and archival vocabulary ('transmit', 'transcript', 'inscrutable'), proving that street narratives can be delivered with academic precision. The rhyme scheme is dense but never forced, relying on the natural cadence of AAVE to bridge the gap between high-art personification and sidewalk reality.
