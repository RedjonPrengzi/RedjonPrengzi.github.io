Producer Notes

Tyler, The Creator constructs a dynamic, improvisational rhythmic pocket by pivoting from standard tail rhymes into a rapid-fire sequence of trochaic proper nouns alternating between nasal coda vowels ('arena' / 'Macarena' / 'Hayna' / 'Jayna') and plosives ('Shayda' / 'Raida' / 'Aida' / 'Raiders').
