Zealots

Fugees (Lauryn Hill)

Rap rejects, my tape deck, ejects projectile Whether Jew or Gentile, I rank top percentile Many styles, more powerful than gamma rays My grammar pays like Carlos Santana plays "Black Magic Woman" So while you fuming, I'm consuming mango juice under Polaris You just embarrassed 'cause it's your last tango in Paris And even after all my logic and my theory I add a "motherfucker" so you ignant niggas hear me

Rhyme Scheme

Complex multisyllabic weaving with internal percussive clusters and triple-rhyme anchors.

Producer Notes

Lauryn Hill demonstrates elite phonetic density by utilizing 'vowel-locking' across scientific and cultural motifs. The transition from the staccato /ɛkt/ cluster ('rejects', 'tape deck', 'ejects') into the fluid /aɪl/ and /eɪz/ chains showcases her ability to shift rhythmic textures without losing structural integrity. Particularly striking is the triple-mosaic rhyme of 'gamma rays', 'grammar pays', and 'Santana plays', which utilizes slant-rhyming to bridge distinct semantic fields. The verse concludes with a classic AAVE phonetic bend, pairing the high-register 'theory' with the colloquial 'hear me' through the shared /ɪəri/ vowel pocket, effectively grounding her intellectual 'logic' in street-level accessibility.
