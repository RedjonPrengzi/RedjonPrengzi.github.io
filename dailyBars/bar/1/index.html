Producer Notes

Nas opens with a highly complex multi-syllabic chain ('monkey'/'funky' and 'flip 'em'/'rhythm'/'kickin'') that mimics a jazz solo's improvisation before settling into a narrative flow. The technical density peaks with the 'Musician'/'inflictin''/'composition' trio, showcasing his ability to weave internal rhymes that serve both rhythmic and narrative purposes.
