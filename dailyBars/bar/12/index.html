Producer Notes

DOOM displays his 'Golden Ear' by exploiting the dropped rhoticity of AAVE, creating a perfect /ɪ-ə/ triplet with 'lingers', 'figures', and the truncated 'invigor.' He maintains structural integrity through seven distinct phonetic pivots, most notably the transition from the long /eɪl/ pocket ('frail', 'fingernail', 'jail') into a final, exhaustive marathon of long /iː/ sounds. By utilizing mosaic rhyming to treat the phrase 'G's of G's' as a phonetic equivalent to 'emcees' and 'sympathy,' DOOM achieves a state of lyrical saturation where the rhyme scheme becomes the primary percussive element of the track.
