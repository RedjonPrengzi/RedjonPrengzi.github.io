Bar #17

Lose Yourself

Eminem

His palms are sweaty, knees weak, arms are heavy There's vomit on his sweater already, mom's spaghetti He's nervous, but on the surface he looks calm and ready To drop bombs, but he keeps on forgetting What he wrote down, the whole crowd goes so loud He opens his mouth, but the words won't come out He's chokin', how, everybody's jokin' now The clock's run out, time's up, over, blaow!

Rhyme Scheme

AABB with multisyllabic compound rhymes.

Producer Notes

Eminem constructs an intense, anxiety-driven structure with two dominant multisyllabic chains. The /ɛdi/ chain (sweaty/heavy/already/spaghetti/ready) anchors lines 1-3 to mirror the escalating physical symptoms. The /aʊt/ chain (down/loud/out/now/blaow) transitions from physical symptoms to psychological paralysis.
