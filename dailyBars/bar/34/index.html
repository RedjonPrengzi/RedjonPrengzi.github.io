Rhyme Scheme

Producer Notes

Cole executes a disciplined three-point anchor scheme across the verse, consistently placing a long 'E' sound (Luigi, Jeezy, Easter) and a short 'U' connector (brother, cover, Bunny) before the terminal rhyme. While the end rhymes share the open /aʊ/ vowel, the rapper relies on slant rhyming to bridge distinct consonant closures, treating nasal /n/, plosive /d/, and open endings as interchangeable within the flow.
