Producer Notes

Denzel Curry constructs a fluid, evolving 24-bar structure that transitions seamlessly between distinct multi-syllabic phonetic blocks across varying line lengths. By pivoting on strict consonant closures—shifting from back-vowel /oʊ/ assonance to slant triple-syllables ('systematic' / 'eyes melted' / 'helpless' / 'Celtics') and concluding with a dense /iːm/ nasal cluster ('dreamin'' / 'demons' / 'redeemin'')—he maintains perpetual momentum without sacrificing narrative clarity.
