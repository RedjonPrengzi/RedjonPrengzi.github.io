Logline

Movies · Entertainment

Judge a movie by its story, not its cover.

I used to spend 30 minutes scrolling Netflix, and hours on trailers, every time I wanted to watch something. Logline shows only the plot: no poster, no cast, no hype. It’s how I found great small films I’d have skipped because I didn’t know the actors.

  • The poster stays hidden until the story hooks you
  • Swipe past what’s not for you
  • Keep a watchlist, and let Logline pick from it when you can’t decide

Ravn

Links · Productivity

Your own raven for the web.

Odin had two ravens, Huginn and Muninn, thought and memory. Every day they flew across the world and brought back what they found. Ravn does the same for you: it gathers the links you find anywhere on your phone and keeps them on your device, ready when you need them.

  • No account, no cloud. Your links stay on your phone
  • Organize links into collections
  • Search your library by title or description, even offline

QuitPilot

Quitting smoking · Health

Not ready to quit yet? Start anyway.

QuitPilot doesn’t wait for your quit day. It meets you while you still smoke and shows what every pack really costs. Then it follows your recovery from the first 20 minutes after your last cigarette. When a craving hits, talk it through with Marcus.

  • Track money saved on smoke-free days
  • Guided breathing when a craving hits
  • A health timeline you can watch improve

Venting · AI companion

Thirteen companions. One of them will get you.

Some days you need someone direct, some days someone warm. Pick the companion that fits, then vent, think out loud, or just talk: Verbalyze listens to your voice too.

Fastual

Fasting · Health

A fasting timer that tells you what the hours mean.

Hour 14 isn’t just hour 14. Fastual shows which phase your fast has likely reached, from digestion to ketosis to autophagy, and explains what’s happening in your body at each stage.

Daily Bars

Rap lyrics · Music

Rap, read like poetry.
