The Philosophy

Clarity is the ultimate feature.

Most apps want your attention. They run feeds, push notifications, and dress up feature bloat as value. I build the opposite: one app per problem, nothing extra, and it gets out of the way once the job is done. Every feature has to earn its place by making the core task easier. The result is software you don't think about. You just use it.

Let's build something that matters.
