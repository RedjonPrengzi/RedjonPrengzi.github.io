A note from the maker

I've spent years trying to convince my friends to quit smoking, and I never managed it. As a non-smoker, I didn't understand how hard it was. When I went looking for apps that could help, I found they all assume you've already quit. I wanted one that helps before you've quit. QuitPilot tracks what smoking costs you and does to your body while you're still doing it, then switches to tracking your recovery when you stop. No lectures, no guilt-tripping, no judgment. When one of my friends actually quit, I made QuitPilot public, hoping it would help more people. I hope it helps you. And if it does, pass it on.

By Redjon, maker of QuitPilot
