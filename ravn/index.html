Your raven for the web

Inspired by Odin's Huginn and Muninn, thought and memory, flying across the web and bringing knowledge back to you.

Save links from any app via share sheet. Organize into collections. Read them offline. No account. No cloud sync. No subscriptions. Just you and your curated web.

Download for iOS

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Share from Anywhere

No more copying and pasting. Save links from Safari, Twitter, Slack, any app, in one tap. Your reading list follows you everywhere.

Collections

Build your own system. Reading List, Tech, Design, Inspiration, however you think, Ravn bends to it. No forced hierarchy, no rigid folders.

Full-Text Search

Find what you saved in seconds, not scroll through hundreds of links. Search by title, domain, or tags. Even works offline.

PIN & Biometric Lock

Your links are yours alone. Lock Ravn behind Face ID, Touch ID, or a PIN. SHA-256 hashed, stored securely, not in the cloud.

Export Anywhere

Your data, your choice. Export everything as JSON, CSV, or Markdown. One click, full portability, no lock-in, ever.

Truly Private

No accounts. No cloud sync. No tracking. Everything stays on your device. Ravn doesn't know what you save, because it never sees it.

"Huginn and Muninn, thought and memory, flew across the world each day and returned with knowledge to share with Odin."

In Norse mythology, ravens were not just messengers. They were intelligence gatherers. They saw what was happening at the edges of the world and brought it back.

Ravn works the same way. Every link you save is a piece of your web, curated, organized, and waiting for you. Not floating in some cloud server. Not fed to an algorithm. Right here, on your device.

Named with love for the Old Norse.
