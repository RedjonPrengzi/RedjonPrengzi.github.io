Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Ravn is designed with privacy at its core. This policy explains what data, if any, is collected and how it is handled.

Data Collection

Ravn stores all data locally on your device. Your links, collections, titles, descriptions, and any other content you save through Ravn never leaves your device. There is no cloud sync, no account system, and no way for us to access your data.

Metadata & Analytics

Ravn uses minimal third-party services that may collect limited, non-personal data:

  • AdMob (Google): If ads are displayed, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad targeting purposes. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • Exponent (Expo Updates): Used to check for app updates. This service may log basic request metadata.

Link Previews

When you save a link, Ravn may fetch OpenGraph metadata (title, description, thumbnail) from the target URL to display a rich preview. This fetch is performed on-device and does not send any of your personal data to third parties beyond what the target website logs in their normal course.

Secure Storage

If you enable PIN lock or biometric authentication, your PIN is hashed using SHA-256 with a unique salt and stored in the device secure enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). The plaintext PIN is never stored.

Data Export

You own your data completely. You can export all saved links and collections at any time in JSON, CSV, or Markdown format via the in-app export feature.

Children's Privacy

Ravn is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Any material changes will be communicated via an update within the app.
