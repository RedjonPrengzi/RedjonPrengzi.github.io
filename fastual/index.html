See what your fast is doing.

A fasting timer that shows which phase your body is in, from digestion to ketosis to autophagy, and tracks your progress over time.

Download for iOS

Get Started in Minutes

Download Fastual

Free on the App Store for iPhone. Open the app and start tracking. No setup.

Set Your Fasting Window

Choose your eating and fasting hours. Start with 16:8 if you're new, or go straight to 20:4 if you're experienced.

Start Your First Fast

Tap start when you finish eating. The timer tracks your progress and notifies you when your window opens. That's it.

A note from the maker

When intermittent fasting was everywhere and everyone was talking about it, I wanted to try it. But I also wanted to learn what it was doing to my body. I figured if I was curious, other people were too, so I built Fastual. It shows which phase your body is in during a fast, from digestion to ketosis to autophagy, and teaches you as you go. I kept it minimal on purpose: no extra features, no fluff. And I made it public because if something is good, it must be shared.

By Redjon, maker of Fastual
