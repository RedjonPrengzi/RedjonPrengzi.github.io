A note from the maker

I didn't build Verbalyze to replace a therapist, and it shouldn't be used as one. It's for the moments you need to talk something through and there's no one awake to call. It's for people who are embarrassed to open up, who don't have a safe space for their thoughts, who live where mental health still carries a stigma. It's for anyone who wants to be heard without having to speak. Growing up, I saw firsthand, in myself, in friends and in family, how much mental health matters. And I saw that sometimes all a person needs is a safe outlet. Verbalyze doesn't promise much, and I don't want to promise anything either. I just hope it's better than nothing. I hope you win the battles no one knows about. I hope you know there's always a way. Stay well.

By Redjon, maker of Verbalyze
