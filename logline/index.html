How it Works

Narrative First

No posters, no actors, and no trailers. Just the plot summary. Judge a movie by its script, not its marketing budget.

Runtime & Mood

Filtering that actually matters. Find exactly what fits your window based on runtime and the specific tone you're looking for.

A note from the maker

I used to spend 30 minutes scrolling Netflix, and hours on trailers, every time I wanted to watch something. Logline shows only the plot: no poster, no cast, no hype. It's how I found great small films I'd have skipped because I didn't know the actors.

By Redjon, maker of Logline
